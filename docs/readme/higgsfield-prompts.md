## One image: a night plate for the social card and profile card

**Idea.** A real half-barrier level crossing at night, lamps lit, seen from the driver's seat, so the drawn crossing in the social card can sit on a photographic ground instead of flat asphalt.

**Model.** Higgsfield Soul (soul_2), prompt only. Soul is the house standard for photographs, it handles low light and practical light sources well, and the plate has to read as a photograph, not an illustration.

**Where it would go.** Behind the drawn crossing in `social.png` and the profile card, darkened to about 35% so the type and the drawing stay on top. It never replaces any terminal output, number or diagram.

**Prompt.**

> Night photograph from a car's driver seat, stopped at a rural European level crossing with an automatic half barrier. The striped red and white barrier arm is down across the near lane only; the far lane is open. Twin red crossing lamps on a black backboard, the left lamp lit, the right lamp dark, the lit lamp throwing red light onto wet asphalt and a white stop line. Railway tracks crossing the road ahead, rails catching a thin line of red. No other cars, no people. Fog low over the fields, black sky, only the red lamp and faint headlight spill. Wide lens, 24mm, eye level, centred road, barrier crossing the frame at one third height. Shot on a full-frame camera at ISO 3200, slight grain, true colour, deep shadows kept black.

**Negative.** No text or lettering on any sign, no logos, no brand names, no number plates, no people, no cartoon or illustration look, no lens flare stars, no blue or purple cast, no green signals, no snow.

**Checks before use.** Barrier covers only one lane; lamps are red, one lit; no legible text anywhere; nothing that looks like a specific real place or company.
